Overview

What is CareerPulse?

CareerPulse is an AI-powered personal career opportunity agent designed to help students and early-career professionals discover relevant internships, jobs, hackathons, workshops, competitions, and other career opportunities.

Instead of manually searching multiple websites every day, CareerPulse continuously searches publicly available information, analyzes opportunities, filters irrelevant results, matches them with your profile, and presents useful opportunities in one place.

Web Search

Searches publicly available web sources for live openings instead of a static job database.

Artificial Intelligence

Reads the actual posting so job descriptions and requirements are understood, not guessed.

Candidate Profiles

Skills, roles, locations, and academic details you control form the matching baseline.

Skill Matching

Compares required and preferred skills to surface matched skills and gaps.

Opportunity Verification

Rejects listing pages, aggregators, expired posts, and duplicates where detectable.

Automation

Discovery and digest scheduling run without manual searching every day.

Personalized Email Briefings

A concise 24-hour digest of news and relevant opportunities delivered to your inbox.

One Place

Saved, interested, and waitlisted opportunities plus your profile live in a single dashboard.

The Problem

Why was CareerPulse built?

Students often have to search across company career pages, job platforms, internship websites, technology communities, hackathon platforms, university websites, and startup websites — every day, in separate places.

CareerPulse was built to bring this process into one personalized system.

Too much time spent searching

Hours lost repeating the same searches across platforms.

Opportunities spread everywhere

No single place shows what is currently open to you.

Deadlines get missed

Short application windows are buried in feeds you rarely revisit.

Irrelevant results everywhere

Generic listings dilute genuinely relevant openings.

The same role repeats

One opportunity appears on many aggregator pages.

Unclear fit

Hard to tell whether you actually match a role before applying.

News and opportunities are disconnected

Technology shifts that affect your career arrive separately from the openings they create.

Classification

Opportunity Categories

Every scored opportunity is placed into one of three existing categories based on the application's matching logic.

🥇

Gold

Strong profile alignment — the majority of required skills are already present in your profile.

🥈

Silver

Moderate alignment — partially matched, with gaps you could reasonably close.

🥉

Bronze

Lower alignment but potentially useful as stretch or awareness opportunities.

These categories are produced by the application's matching logic and are intended as guidance, not guarantees of selection, interviews, or offers.

24-Hour Digest

Technology News Digest

CareerPulse can send one automated digest every 24 hours containing major technology developments from the previous 24 hours.

The system prioritizes genuinely significant stories rather than filling the email with trivial news, and includes verified source links where available.

Direct Outreach

Email Drafts You Approve

CareerPulse can help generate a professional outreach or application email based on a specific opportunity. You stay in control of every send.

Important

  • CareerPulse never automatically sends an email to a recruiter or company merely because a job was discovered.
  • You must explicitly approve sending.
  • Recruiter email addresses are never guessed — only publicly indexed contacts are surfaced.

Privacy & Security

Responsible handling of credentials and data

How CareerPulse handles configuration and user information in its current implementation.

API keys stay on the backend

Search and AI keys are read server-side and never shipped to the browser.

SMTP credentials are not exposed

Mail credentials are used only by the backend mail service.

OAuth handled server-side

The authentication exchange happens on the server; the browser only receives a session.

Public web information only

Opportunity and news discovery uses publicly available sources.

You control your profile

Profile fields are editable and are stored against your own account.

No automatic outreach sends

Outreach emails are only sent after your explicit approval.
