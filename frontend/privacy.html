Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what CareerPulse collects, why it collects it, and what you can control. It describes the application as it is actually built today.

Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you sign in and build your CareerPulse profile. This can include:

  • Account and authentication details used to sign in, such as your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Professional profile information, such as your headline, skills, areas of interest, work experience, and links such as GitHub and LinkedIn.
  • Education details you choose to provide, such as degree, college and year.
  • Your location and preferred working location.
  • Career preferences, such as preferred roles, preferred domains and experience level.
  • Opportunities you save, and outreach emails CareerPulse sends on your behalf, including the recipient, subject and message content.
  • Replies received to that outreach, when you have connected your mailbox.

We also process information retrieved from public web sources while searching for opportunities and technology news, and information returned by the third-party services described below.

How We Use Information

CareerPulse uses your information to:

  • Search publicly available web information for opportunities relevant to your profile.
  • Rank and personalise those opportunities for you.
  • Find and summarise relevant technology news.
  • Prepare outreach emails and send them to recruiters on your behalf when you ask it to.
  • Track replies to that outreach, so you can see who responded and what they said.
  • Keep your profile, saved opportunities and application history available to you.

Gmail / Google Account Access

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you choose to connect one, CareerPulse uses Google OAuth and requests read-only access to Gmail. It does not request permission to send, modify, or delete your mail.

The connected mailbox is used only to identify and read email replies that relate to CareerPulse outreach, so it can match a reply to the application it belongs to and show you the response.

The Gmail access and refresh tokens issued by Google are stored server-side in encrypted form. They are never displayed to you and are never sent to your browser. You can disconnect the integration at any time, which deletes the stored connection and its tokens.

Sending email is a separate feature. CareerPulse sends outreach email over SMTP using its own sending configuration. The Gmail connection is not used to send email, and is not required in order for CareerPulse to send on your behalf.

AI and Third-Party Services

CareerPulse relies on third-party services to provide its functionality. They process data only as needed to do so:

  • Supabase is used for authentication and database storage, including your profile, saved opportunities, applications and mailbox connection.
  • Tavily may be used for web search and page retrieval, to find opportunities and technology news from public sources.
  • Google Gemini may be used to analyse, summarise, extract and personalise opportunity and news information.
  • Google OAuth and the Gmail API are used only if you connect a Google account, as described above.
  • An SMTP mail server is used to send outreach email you request.

Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. Query text sent to search and AI services can include information from your profile, such as your skills, interests and preferred roles, so that results can be personalised to you.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a Supabase database and is intended to be isolated between accounts: information saved under one CareerPulse account is not intended to be visible to another account.

Mailbox OAuth tokens are encrypted before they are written to the database, so a raw database read does not yield a usable Gmail credential. CareerPulse communicates with its own backend over authenticated requests, and Gmail tokens stay on the server rather than in the browser.

No system can guarantee absolute security. You should not use CareerPulse with an account password you also use elsewhere.

Data Retention and Deletion

CareerPulse retains your profile, saved opportunities, applications and tracked replies while your account is active, so the service can work as intended.

You can disconnect and delete your mailbox connection yourself at any time. You can also ask us to delete your account and the personal data associated with it, by using the contact details below. On request we will remove your profile and stored records, subject to any records we are required to retain.

User Choices

  • Update or remove information in your profile at any time.
  • Remove saved opportunities and your application history.
  • Connect or disconnect a Google/Gmail account at any time. Disconnecting deletes the stored connection and its tokens.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Do not connect a mailbox at all. Reply tracking is optional; the rest of CareerPulse works without it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the application changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version, and the updated policy will be published on this page.
